Features

Everything your team needs to ship faster

From raw carrier PDFs to sorted, assigned label packets — Warehub handles the entire workflow automatically.

⚡

Instant PDF Parsing

Drop in a PDF from any carrier. Warehub extracts every tracking number, detects the carrier, and groups labels automatically — no manual entry.

🗂️

Auto-Sort by Carrier

Labels are automatically sorted by carrier and printed with divider pages, so packers know exactly what they're handling at a glance.

👥

Packer Assignment

Assign shipments to specific packers. Print by packer to give each team member exactly their portion of the day's orders.

🖨️

Missing Label Detection

Warehub flags any labels that couldn't be parsed and surfaces them with thumbnails so you can reprint individually or all at once.

📦

FBA Shipment Manager

Parse Amazon FBA box labels, track shipments by FNSKU and ASIN, and maintain a reprint queue for missing box labels.

📊

Daily Stats & History

Track orders processed, labels printed, and packer performance over time. Built-in log keeps a full history of every import batch.

🔒

Team Access Control

Role-based PIN system for packers and managers. Managers get full access; packers see only their assigned work queue.

☁️

Cloud Sync

All data syncs to the cloud so your team can access the same records from any device, any shift, without losing history.

🌐

No Install Required

Runs entirely in the browser — open the app, log in, and you're working. No software to install, no IT team needed.

How it works

From PDF to printed labels in 3 steps

Warehub fits into your existing workflow. No new hardware, no complex setup.

1

Upload your carrier PDF

Drag and drop the shipping label PDF from your carrier portal — UPS, FedEx, USPS, or Amazon. Any size batch.

2

Warehub sorts & assigns

Labels are parsed, grouped by carrier, and assigned to packers automatically. Missing labels are flagged instantly.

3

Print & pack

Hit print. Each packer gets their sorted packet with carrier dividers. Done — your team can start packing immediately.
