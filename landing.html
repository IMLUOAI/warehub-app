Features

One job, done in the browser

No install. Warehub does not buy postage and does not change Amazon orders.

1

Read the label PDF

Each page is read for tracking number, carrier, SKU, and shelf. UPS Ground Saver pages that also show a USPS final-mile number stay in the UPS stack.

2

Print by carrier

Pages are grouped into UPS, FedEx, and USPS stacks. A page that cannot be read is kept in Missing Labels with its image so it can be printed by hand.

3

Pack by scan

The pack station scan matches the label barcode to that page and shows the SKU and shelf from the label. A scan that matches two orders is rejected instead of completing the wrong one.

4

Account data

Sign-in is per account. Batches and pack records stay with that account. Export or delete a batch from the app. Details are in the privacy policy.

How it works

Four steps

1

Upload

Drop the carrier label PDF you already printed.

2

Review

Check carrier counts and the missing-label queue before printing.

3

Print

Print the carrier stacks. Reprint any missing page from its image.

4

Scan

Scan the label at the pack station. Confirm the SKU matches the label, then pack.
