Privacy policy

Effective 6 October 2026. Warehub is operated from 1701 10th St Suite 200, Plano, TX 75074. Contact hello@wareplatform.com.

What this app does

Warehub reads shipping-label PDFs that a seller uploads, including tracking numbers, carrier names, SKUs, shelves, and the recipient name and address printed on the label. It does not buy postage and it does not change Amazon orders.

Data we store

Account name and email from the sign-in provider. Subscription status from Stripe. Label batches, pack records, and optional floor records in our Cloudflare database, tied to the account. Payment card numbers are handled by Stripe, not stored by Warehub.

Why

To provide the label sort, print, and pack-scan service the account subscribed to, to bill that account, and to answer support requests.

Sharing

Sign-in is processed by Clerk. Payments are processed by Stripe. Hosting and storage are on Cloudflare. We do not sell label data. We disclose data if required by law.

Retention and deletion

A seller can delete a batch in the app. To delete the account and stored batches, email hello@wareplatform.com. We delete account data within 30 days unless a law requires a longer hold.

Amazon

Warehub is not an Amazon service. If a seller connects an Amazon selling account later, Warehub will request only the API roles named on the listing, and this page will be updated before that connection is offered.
